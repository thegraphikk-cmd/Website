Google Business Profile video verification: what to record

What to show in a Google Business Profile verification video, how to record it for a shop or a home-based service business, and what to do if Google rejects it.

Most new Google Business Profiles in India are now verified by video. You record a short clip on your phone showing that your business is real and that you manage it, and Google reviews it. It's simple, but many videos get rejected for missing one piece of evidence. Here's what to show.

The basic rules

Google's guidance for video verification generally requires:

  • One continuous recording, with no cuts or editing.
  • Recorded and uploaded from your phone through the Google Business Profile app or Google Maps.
  • A reasonable length: long enough to show everything clearly, without rushing.

Check the instructions shown in your own profile before recording, since requirements can vary by business type.

The three things your video must prove

1. Location

Show where the business is. Start outside: the street name board, the building, nearby landmarks or shops, and your signboard. Pan slowly so the reviewer can match it to your address on the map.

2. Business activity

Show that a real business operates. Film your equipment, stock, workspace, service counter, branded items, or work in progress.

3. Management

Show that you control the business. For example, unlock the shop shutter or office door, open the cash counter or a staff-only area, or show business documents such as your shop licence, GST certificate or a utility bill in the business name.

For a shop, clinic or office

  1. Start on the street with the road name or a landmark visible.
  2. Walk to your entrance and show the signboard.
  3. Unlock or open the door.
  4. Walk through the space showing products, equipment or the reception area.
  5. Finish with a business document on the counter.

For a home-based or service-area business

If customers don't visit you, Google still needs proof of the area and the business, but your address won't be shown publicly.

  1. Show your street or area (a road sign or landmark).
  2. Show the tools of your trade: a branded vehicle, equipment, work samples or your workstation.
  3. Show management proof, such as unlocking your branded vehicle or showing business documents in your name.

Designers, consultants and other online businesses can show their workstation, branded materials and business documents.

What to avoid

  • Editing, cutting or adding music
  • Filming only the inside, with no location context
  • Showing other people's faces, bank details, Aadhaar or other sensitive ID numbers
  • Recording in darkness or with a shaky, too-fast pan
  • Using an address that's a virtual office or co-working desk you don't actually operate from

If your video is rejected

Google tells you what was missing, such as "location not visible" or "no proof of management". Don't record the same video again. Record a new one that includes everything from the first, plus clearer evidence for the missing point. Reviews can take several business days, so avoid editing the profile while waiting.
