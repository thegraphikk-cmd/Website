You've created a Google Business Profile, but when you search, your business isn't there. Or it appears for your name but never for searches like "bakery near me". These are two different problems with different fixes. Start by finding out which one you have.

First: test properly

Search in an incognito window, or ask a friend on another phone, so your own browsing doesn't skew the results.

  • Search your exact business name plus area. If you don't appear at all, it's usually a verification, suspension or duplicate problem (fixes 1–4).
  • If you appear for your name but not for general searches, your profile is live but not ranking yet (fixes 5–9).

1. The profile isn't verified

Unverified profiles don't show publicly. Open your profile at business.google.com and check for a "Get verified" or "Verification pending" message. Complete whatever method is offered. In India this is often a short video of your location and business. If a video was rejected, read the reason and record a new one that fixes it.

2. Verification is still under review

Reviews can take several business days. Avoid editing your name, address or category during this time, because edits can restart the review.

3. The profile is suspended

Look for a suspension notice in your dashboard. Common causes are keywords in the business name, a virtual office or shared address, a home address shown publicly for a service business, or too many edits at once. Fix the cause, then submit an appeal through Google's reinstatement form with proof such as a utility bill, GST certificate or shop licence.

4. There's a duplicate listing

If someone, perhaps an old employee or an earlier version of you, created another listing for the same business, Google may hide one. Search Maps for variations of your name and phone number. Request ownership of the duplicate, then ask Google to mark it as a duplicate or close it.

5. Your category is too general

"Store" or "Service" won't rank for anything specific. Choose the most precise primary category, like "Bridal shop" or "Physiotherapist", and add secondary categories for other services.

6. Your service area is unrealistic

If you've added half of Tamil Nadu as your service area, Google still ranks you mainly by your actual base location. Keep service areas to places you genuinely serve, and don't expect to rank in a city far from your address.

7. The profile is thin

A verified profile with no photos, no services, no hours and two reviews will lose to competitors who filled everything in. Add services with descriptions, at least ten real photos, accurate hours and a description that says what you do and where.

8. You have few or old reviews

Review count, rating and how recent they are all matter. Ask every satisfied customer for a review with your direct review link, and reply to each one.

9. No website, or a weak one

Google uses your website to understand your business. A profile with no website, or a link to an Instagram page, usually ranks below competitors with a proper, fast site that has a page for each service and your address on it. Make sure the name, address and phone on your site exactly match the profile.

Still not showing?

Local rankings depend heavily on where the searcher is. You might rank first for people on your street and not appear at all three kilometres away. That's normal. Improving reviews, your website and your profile widens the area where you show up, but no business ranks everywhere.
