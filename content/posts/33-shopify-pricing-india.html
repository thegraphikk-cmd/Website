Shopify's pricing page shows one number per plan, but your real monthly bill includes GST, transaction fees, payment gateway charges and apps. Here's what a Shopify store actually costs an Indian business in 2026.

Short answer: most small Indian stores spend about ₹2,500 to ₹6,000 a month on Shopify all-in (plan, GST, a few apps), plus payment gateway charges of around 2% of sales. Prices below are as listed in late 2026, before GST; Shopify changes them occasionally, so check shopify.com/in/pricing before you commit.

Yearly billing saves roughly a quarter compared with paying month to month. Shopify Plus, for large brands, starts at well over ₹1 lakh a month.

The costs that aren't on the pricing page

1. GST

Add 18% GST to the plan price. Basic at about ₹1,499 becomes roughly ₹1,770 a month. If you're GST-registered, you can usually claim this as input tax credit.

2. Shopify's transaction fee

Shopify Payments isn't available in India, so you use a third-party gateway such as Razorpay, Cashfree, PayU or PhonePe. Shopify then charges its own fee on each order: around 2% on Basic, 1% on Grow and about 0.5 to 0.6% on Advanced. Cash-on-delivery orders marked as manual payments are generally not charged this fee.

3. Payment gateway charges

Your gateway charges separately, typically around 2% per transaction for cards, UPI and netbanking on standard plans, plus GST on the fee. So on Basic, an online-paid order can cost you about 4% in combined fees. That's the main reason growing stores move to Grow. We cover gateway options in UPI, cards or COD: setting up payments.

4. Apps

Many useful features come from apps: reviews, WhatsApp notifications, COD verification, shipping, GST invoices. Several are free or under ₹500 a month, but it's easy to end up with five paid apps at ₹1,000 to ₹3,000 a month combined. Apps also slow your store down; see how to speed up a Shopify store on mobile.

5. Theme

Free themes are good enough for many stores. Premium themes are a one-time cost, usually around $180 to $400 (roughly ₹15,000 to ₹35,000).

6. Domain and email

A .com or .in domain costs a few hundred to around a thousand rupees a year. Shopify doesn't host email, so business email is extra; see business email on your own domain.

Fees scale with your sales, so the plan price matters less than it looks. Once your online sales reach a few lakh a month, Grow's lower transaction fee usually pays for itself.

Which plan should you start on?

  • Basic for almost every new store. You can upgrade any time without rebuilding.
  • Grow once online-paid sales are high enough that the fee difference (about 1% of sales) beats the extra plan cost, or you need more staff accounts.
  • Starter only if you sell through Instagram or WhatsApp and don't need a real storefront yet.

Is Shopify worth it compared with WooCommerce?

Shopify costs more each month but needs almost no technical upkeep. WooCommerce has no platform fee, but you pay for hosting, maintenance and more developer time. We compare them in Shopify vs WooCommerce for Indian brands, and the full build cost in how much an e-commerce website costs in India.
