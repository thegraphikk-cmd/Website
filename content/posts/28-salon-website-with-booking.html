Salons and beauty studios often live on Instagram, and for good reason: the work is visual. But when a customer is ready to book, especially for a bridal package or an expensive treatment, they want prices, availability and reassurance. A focused website turns Instagram interest into confirmed bookings.

What customers want to know before booking

  • What services do you offer, and what do they cost?
  • Where are you, and when are you open?
  • Who will do my hair, skin or makeup, and are they good?
  • What do other customers say?
  • How do I book, and can I choose a time?

Pages a salon website needs

Home

Your style in one striking photo, your location, timings and a big "Book on WhatsApp" button.

Service menu with prices

The most important page. List services by category, such as hair, skin, nails and makeup, with durations and prices or "from" prices. Customers who can see prices book faster and ask fewer questions.

Bridal and special packages

Bridal makeup and pre-bridal packages deserve their own page with what's included, trial details, pricing, an enquiry form and plenty of photos. This is often where the most valuable bookings come from.

Gallery

Your best work, organised by service. Use your Instagram photos, but pick the strongest and compress them so the page stays fast.

Team

Stylists and artists with photos and specialties. Many customers book a specific person.

Reviews

Show real Google reviews and encourage new ones after each visit.

Booking: WhatsApp or an online system?

WhatsApp booking works well for most single-location salons. A button opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message such as "Hi, I'd like to book a haircut on ___". Your front desk confirms the slot. It's free and customers are comfortable with it. We explain how in our guide to adding a WhatsApp button.

An online booking system makes sense when you have several stylists, high volumes, or want customers to see live availability and pay a deposit. Choose one that sends reminders, which cuts no-shows.

Get found on Google Maps

"Salon near me" and "bridal makeup artist in [area]" are high-intent searches. To show up:

  • Complete your Google Business Profile with services, prices, photos and hours.
  • Use the right primary category: "Beauty salon", "Hair salon", "Makeup artist" or "Spa".
  • Keep name, address and phone identical on your website and profile.
  • Ask happy customers for reviews every day, not occasionally.

Common mistakes

  • No prices at all, just "DM for price"
  • A gallery of huge, uncompressed photos that takes ages to load
  • Menu uploaded as a blurry image that can't be read on a phone
  • Timings or prices out of date
