Fashion is one of the most popular categories for Indian online stores, and one of the hardest. Shoppers can't touch the fabric or try the fit, so returns are high, especially with COD. The brands that do well online answer fit and fabric questions so clearly that customers order the right size the first time.

Size charts that actually help

  • Give garment measurements (chest, waist, length, sleeve) in inches and centimetres, not just S/M/L.
  • Explain how to measure with a simple diagram.
  • Make charts product-specific: a kurti and a blouse don't fit the same way.
  • Add fit notes: "Relaxed fit, size down if between sizes" or "Runs small".
  • Show the model's height and size worn in every photo set.

For sarees, dupattas and fabric, give length, width, blouse piece details and fabric weight.

Photos that show the real thing

  • Front, back, side and close-ups of fabric, embroidery, print and border.
  • On a model or mannequin, plus a flat lay.
  • True colours in daylight. Mention that shades can vary slightly by screen.
  • A short video of the fabric moving helps a lot for ethnic wear.

Describe the fabric and care

Fabric (pure cotton, cotton blend, georgette, silk mark if applicable), lining, transparency, stretch, wash care and whether colours may bleed in the first wash. Honest details prevent "not as expected" returns.

Returns and exchanges

  • Exchange-first policies (size exchange free, refunds as store credit or with conditions) protect margins while staying customer-friendly.
  • Be clear on the window, conditions (unworn, tags attached) and exceptions (innerwear, customised or altered items).
  • Make the process simple: a form or WhatsApp, with reverse pickup where possible.

Handle COD carefully

COD is popular for fashion, but refused deliveries (RTO) are costly. Confirm COD orders by WhatsApp or call before dispatch, offer a small prepaid discount, and consider COD limits for high-value orders. See cash on delivery and RTO.

Store setup tips

  • Use variants for sizes and colours, with photos switching per colour.
  • Show stock per size and "Only 2 left" honestly.
  • Add filters for size, colour, fabric, occasion and price.
  • Offer "Notify me" for sold-out sizes.
  • Collections by occasion work well: festive, office wear, wedding guest.

Festive seasons

Diwali, Pongal, Onam, wedding season and end-of-season sales drive a big share of fashion sales. Plan stock, shoots and offers weeks ahead, and make sure your site is fast enough for the traffic; see speeding up a Shopify store.
