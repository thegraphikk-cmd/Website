Property buyers research for weeks before they call. They compare projects, study floor plans, check RERA numbers and look for honest pricing, mostly on their phones. A real estate website that makes this easy turns that research into site visits. One that hides prices behind "Call for details" and loads slowly loses buyers to the builder down the road.

Who this is for

  • Builders and developers with one or several projects.
  • Property consultants and channel partners listing projects from different builders.
  • Plot and villa developers selling layouts.

The core needs are the same: clear project information, trust, and fast lead handling.

Every project needs its own page

Don't put all projects on one long page. Each project deserves a dedicated page with:

  • Location with a map and distances to schools, hospitals, IT parks and metro stations.
  • Configurations: 2BHK, 3BHK, plot sizes, with carpet area clearly stated.
  • Floor plans as images, not just a downloadable PDF.
  • Price range: "Starting ₹68 lakh*" builds more trust and better leads than no price at all.
  • Amenities, specifications and construction status, with recent site photos.
  • RERA registration number and a link to the state RERA website.
  • Brochure download and a clear enquiry button.

A separate page per project also helps you rank for searches like "3BHK flats in Porur" or the project's own name.

RERA details

Under RERA, registered projects generally need to display their registration number in advertisements, and that includes your website. Show it prominently on each project page, along with the agent registration number if you're a consultant. Check your state RERA authority's current rules. It's a legal requirement, and buyers increasingly look for it.

Make the next step easy

  • Book a site visit form with preferred date and time.
  • WhatsApp button with a pre-filled message that names the project; see how to set one up.
  • Click-to-call on every project page.
  • Brochure download that asks only for a name and phone number.

Shorter forms get more leads. You can qualify buyers on the call.

Respond within minutes

Property leads go cold fast because buyers enquire with several builders at once. Send website enquiries straight to WhatsApp or a CRM your sales team checks constantly, with the project name included. The builder who calls back in ten minutes usually gets the site visit.

Photos, videos and virtual tours

  • Use real photos of completed projects and current construction, dated where possible.
  • Label renders as "Artist's impression".
  • Walkthrough videos and 360° tours help NRI and out-of-city buyers, but host videos on YouTube and embed them so they don't slow the page.

Speed matters more than you think

Real estate sites are often heavy with large renders, sliders and videos. On mobile data, a slow site loses buyers before they see a floor plan. Compress images, avoid auto-playing videos, and test on a real phone. See what a fast site actually needs.

Running ads to your site

Most builders run Google and Meta ads for launches. Send ad traffic to a focused project landing page, not your home page. We explain why in landing page vs website for ads.

Build trust

  • Completed projects with photos and handover dates.
  • Customer testimonials, ideally video.
  • Bank approvals and loan tie-ups.
  • Years in business and the team behind the company.
  • Google reviews; see how to get more of them.

A simple structure

  1. Home: ongoing projects, trust points, enquiry form
  2. Projects: one page each (ongoing, upcoming, completed)
  3. About and completed work
  4. Contact and site-visit booking

Start with this and add NRI pages, blogs and calculators once the basics are working well.
