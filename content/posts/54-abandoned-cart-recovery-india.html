Most people who add something to their cart never finish buying. Industry estimates often put the abandonment rate at around 70%. Some of those shoppers were just browsing, but many were close to buying and got stopped by something you can fix. And some can be brought back with a simple reminder.

Why shoppers abandon carts

  • Unexpected costs: shipping, COD fees or taxes that appear only at checkout.
  • Forced account creation before buying.
  • Long or slow checkout, especially on mobile data.
  • Missing payment options: no UPI or no COD.
  • Payment failures, UPI timeouts or OTP problems.
  • Trust doubts: unclear returns, no contact details, an unfamiliar brand.
  • Delivery time too long or not shown.
  • Just comparing, or got distracted.

Step 1: Fix the causes

Show costs early

Mention shipping charges and free-shipping thresholds on product pages and in the cart, not just at the final step. If you charge for COD, say so upfront.

Allow guest checkout

Let people buy with just a phone number and address. You can offer an account after the order.

Keep checkout short

  • Ask only for what you need.
  • Auto-fill city and state from the PIN code.
  • Use the right keyboard on mobile (numbers for phone and PIN).
  • Make sure checkout loads fast on a mid-range phone; see speeding up a Shopify store.

Offer the payment methods people expect

UPI, cards, netbanking and COD cover most Indian shoppers. One-click or saved-details checkout options can help further. See setting up payments for an Indian website.

Build trust at checkout

  • Short return-policy summary near the pay button.
  • Contact number or WhatsApp link.
  • Reviews on product pages.
  • Clear delivery estimates by PIN code.

Step 2: Send reminders

When a shopper enters their phone or email and leaves, you can remind them. Platforms like Shopify include abandoned checkout emails, and apps add SMS and WhatsApp.

A simple sequence

  1. After about an hour: a friendly reminder with the product photo and a link back to their cart.
  2. After about a day: answer common doubts: returns, delivery time, COD available.
  3. After two or three days: optionally, a small incentive like free shipping. Use this sparingly, or customers learn to wait for discounts.

WhatsApp works well in India

WhatsApp reminders often get far more opens than email. They need the WhatsApp Business API through a provider, and customers should have agreed to receive messages. Keep them short and helpful, never spammy.

Step 3: Handle failed payments

Some "abandoned" carts are actually failed UPI or card payments. Make it easy to retry with a different method, and consider a quick WhatsApp message offering help or COD.

Measure it

  • Track your checkout completion rate monthly.
  • Watch where people drop off: cart, shipping, or payment step.
  • Track how many abandoned checkouts your reminders recover.

Small improvements add up. Recovering even a few extra orders a week can pay for your store's running costs.
