Couples usually find wedding photographers on Instagram, then visit their website to decide. The website is where they see full wedding stories, check packages and send an enquiry. A great website turns a "nice photos" moment into a booked date.

Curate, don't dump

Showing every photo you've ever taken makes it harder for couples to see your style. Pick your best 30 to 60 images for the main portfolio, chosen to show:

  • Candid emotion and storytelling.
  • Portraits of the couple.
  • Rituals and ceremonies across the cultures you shoot (Tamil, Telugu, North Indian, Christian, Muslim weddings and more).
  • Different light: daylight, indoor halls, night receptions.

Full wedding stories

Couples want to see a whole wedding, not just highlights. Create a page per featured wedding: a short story, 40 to 80 images in order (pre-wedding, haldi or mehendi, ceremony, reception), the venue and city, and a short film if you shoot video. These pages also rank for searches like "wedding photography at [venue name]".

Show your packages

Couples are planning budgets. Listing packages, or at least "starting from" prices, gets better-fit enquiries:

  • Coverage hours and number of events.
  • Photographers and videographers included.
  • Deliverables: edited photos, albums, films, reels, drone.
  • Delivery timelines.
  • Travel for destination weddings.

Make enquiring easy

  • A short form: names, wedding dates, events, city and venue, budget range, phone.
  • A WhatsApp button. Many couples prefer to start there.
  • A quick reply. Popular dates get booked fast.

Speed: the big challenge

Photographer websites are often painfully slow because galleries load dozens of full-resolution images. Couples browsing on phones give up before the photos appear. Fix it by:

  • Exporting web versions (around 2000 px, compressed, WebP).
  • Lazy-loading gallery images below the first screen.
  • Embedding films from YouTube or Vimeo instead of uploading video files.

See testing your website speed.

Protect your images, reasonably

Small watermarks or right-click blocking won't stop determined copying, but they can discourage casual reuse. Lower-resolution web images are the best protection. Deliver full-resolution files only through private client galleries.

Trust and social proof

  • Testimonials from couples, ideally with a photo from their wedding.
  • Google reviews; see how to get more.
  • Features in wedding blogs or magazines.
  • An about page with your face and story. Couples are inviting you into a very personal day.

Be found

  • A Google Business Profile with your best work.
  • Titles like "Candid Wedding Photographer in Chennai | [Your Name]".
  • Listings on wedding platforms that send you enquiries.
