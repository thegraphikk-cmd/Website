If you serve customers across Chennai, Tamil Nadu or several Indian cities, you want to appear when someone searches "your service + their area". The tempting shortcut is to copy one page fifty times and swap the city name. Google calls these doorway pages, and they rarely work for long. Here's the approach that does.

First: how Google decides local results

For searches with local intent, Google weighs three things:

  • Relevance: does your business match the search?
  • Distance: how close are you to the searcher or the area they named?
  • Prominence: reviews, links, mentions and overall reputation.

Distance is the hardest to influence. The Google Maps results usually favour businesses physically near the searcher. Location pages mainly help in the regular (organic) results.

If you have branches: one profile and page each

Each real, staffed location should have:

  • Its own Google Business Profile.
  • Its own page on your website with that branch's address, phone, hours, map, photos, team and reviews.
  • The Business Profile linking to that branch's page, not the home page.

This is the strongest setup for local ranking.

If you travel to customers: service areas

Service-area businesses (home services, event vendors, mobile clinics, installers) should set service areas in their Business Profile and hide the address if customers don't visit. Then build location pages for the main areas you genuinely serve.

What makes a location page useful

Each page should be worth reading on its own. Include details specific to that area:

  • Projects or customers in that area, with photos: "Modular kitchen in a Porur apartment".
  • Reviews from customers there.
  • Local specifics: travel time, availability, pricing differences, local regulations or common problems in that area.
  • Who serves the area: the local team or partner.
  • FAQs customers there actually ask.
  • A clear way to contact you, with area-specific details if you have them.

If you can't write at least a few genuinely different paragraphs for an area, it probably doesn't need its own page yet. Cover it on a broader regional page instead.

Doorway page warning signs

  • Dozens of near-identical pages where only the city name changes.
  • Pages for areas you don't actually serve.
  • Pages that exist only for search engines and aren't linked from your menu or content.
  • Keyword-stuffed text like "best plumber Adyar plumber in Adyar Adyar plumbing".

These can drag down how Google sees your whole site.

Structure that works

  • A "Areas we serve" page linking to each location page.
  • Location pages linking to relevant service pages, and service pages listing the areas they cover.
  • Clear titles: "Interior Designer in Velachery, Chennai | Your Brand".
  • Location pages included in your sitemap.

Build prominence in each area

  • Collect reviews from customers in each area, mentioning the work.
  • Get listed with consistent details; see free business listings.
  • Earn mentions from local associations, suppliers and community sites.

Start small
