A quotation from sales@yourbusiness.in looks very different from one sent by yourbusiness2019@gmail.com. Business email on your own domain costs very little and makes you look established. Here's how the main options compare for Indian businesses.

Why it's worth it

  • Trust: customers, suppliers and banks take a domain email more seriously.
  • Deliverability: properly set-up domain email is less likely to land in spam.
  • Control: when an employee leaves, you keep their mailbox and its history.
  • Consistency: your email matches your website and your visiting card.

The three main options

Prices below are approximate per-user monthly rates in late 2026, on yearly billing, before 18% GST. Both providers change plans from time to time, so check their pricing pages before buying.

1. Google Workspace

Gmail on your domain, plus Google Drive, Meet, Calendar and Docs.

  • Starter: about ₹270 per user (₹325 on flexible monthly billing), 30 GB per user.
  • Standard: about ₹1,080 per user, with much more storage and Meet recording.
  • A lower-priced Base tier has also been offered in India for very small teams.

Best for: teams who already live in Gmail and Google Drive, and want the most familiar experience on Android phones.

2. Zoho Mail

An Indian company with ad-free email, good mobile apps and lower prices.

  • Mail Lite: commonly listed at around ₹59 to ₹90 per user, with 5 to 10 GB.
  • Mail Premium: around ₹199 per user, with more storage and archiving.
  • Zoho has offered a free plan for a few users on one domain, though limits have changed over time.

Best for: small businesses that mainly need reliable email and want to keep costs low.

3. Email from your hosting company

Many hosting plans include email accounts at no extra cost.

  • Pros: cheapest, already paid for.
  • Cons: weaker spam filtering, small mailboxes, poorer apps, and if your website host has problems, your email goes down too.

Best for: a single address that receives website enquiries and isn't critical.

How many mailboxes do you need?

You pay per user, not per address. Most providers let you add free aliases and groups, so one person can receive mail for info@, sales@ and accounts@ without paying for three mailboxes. A five-person business might need only two or three paid users.

Setting it up properly

Whichever you choose, the setup at your domain registrar matters:

  • MX records point your domain's email to Google or Zoho.
  • SPF, DKIM and DMARC records prove your emails are genuine. Without them, your quotations and invoices are more likely to land in spam. Gmail and other big providers now expect these for reliable delivery.
  • Make sure your website's contact form sends through a verified address too, or enquiries can go missing.

It's a one-time job, but a common place for mistakes. If you're registering a new domain, see how to choose a domain name first.

Our recommendation

  • One or two people, tight budget: Zoho Mail Lite.
  • A team that shares documents and meets online: Google Workspace Starter.
  • Just a contact address for the website: hosting email or a forwarder can be enough.
