Most home bakers and small cake shops start on Instagram and WhatsApp, and many are flooded with the same questions every day: price for 1 kg, eggless available, delivery to my area, can you do this design? A simple website with your menu, prices and an order form answers most of them and turns DMs into confirmed orders.

What customers want to know

  • Flavours, sizes and prices per weight.
  • Eggless, vegan or sugar-free options.
  • How far in advance to order, and delivery areas and charges.
  • Photos of past cakes, especially custom designs.
  • How to pay and confirm.

The pages you need

  1. Menu with prices: cakes by flavour and weight, cupcakes, brownies, cookies, hampers.
  2. Custom cakes: a gallery and an enquiry form (date, weight, flavour, theme, reference photo upload, budget).
  3. Order online: for standard items, with delivery date and time slot.
  4. Delivery and pickup: areas, charges, cut-off times.
  5. About: your story and kitchen. Customers love the person behind a home bakery.
  6. Contact: WhatsApp, phone, pickup address if any.

Order flow options

Simple: menu + WhatsApp order

Each item has an "Order on WhatsApp" button that opens a chat with the cake, weight and date filled in. You confirm and share a payment link. Great to start; see adding WhatsApp to your site.

Full: online ordering with payment

Customers choose the product, weight, date and slot, and pay online. Use date pickers that block fully booked days and enforce your notice period (for example, 48 hours for custom cakes). See when to move from WhatsApp to an online store.

Take advance payment

For custom cakes, take full or part payment upfront through UPI or a payment link. It cuts no-shows and last-minute cancellations. State your cancellation and refund policy clearly.

Show your FSSAI details

Food businesses in India, including home bakers, generally need FSSAI registration or a licence depending on scale. Display your FSSAI number on the website and packaging. It builds trust and is expected for food businesses; check the current requirements for your turnover.

Photos sell cakes

  • Natural light, clean background, a slice shot showing layers.
  • Group the gallery by occasion: birthdays, weddings, kids' themes, anniversaries.
  • Compress images so the gallery loads fast; see product photos with a phone.

Connect Instagram

Keep posting on Instagram, but put your website link in your bio and story highlights for "Menu", "Prices" and "How to order". Your site handles the questions; Instagram handles the inspiration. See turning followers into sales.

Be found locally

  • Set up a Google Business Profile, as a service-area business if you deliver from home.
  • Use clear titles: "Eggless Custom Cakes in Velachery, Chennai".
  • Ask happy customers for Google reviews.

Festivals like Christmas, New Year and Diwali are peak seasons. Get your site ready a few weeks before, with clear cut-off dates for orders.
