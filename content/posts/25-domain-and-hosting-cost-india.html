Every website has two running costs: the domain (your address on the internet) and hosting (where the website's files live). Both are cheaper than many people expect, but intro offers and renewal prices make them confusing. Here's what a typical Indian business actually pays.

What a small business actually needs

A typical small business site with a few hundred to a few thousand visitors a month doesn't need expensive hosting. What matters is:

  • Speed: servers in or near India, or a CDN, and caching.
  • Free SSL: the padlock and https, now standard.
  • Automatic backups you can restore.
  • Decent support that responds when the site is down.

Avoid the very cheapest shared plans for business sites. They often pack hundreds of sites onto one server, which makes yours slow.

Renewal traps to avoid

  • Intro prices that triple on renewal. Compare the renewal price, or buy a longer term at the intro price if you're sure.
  • Paid add-ons you don't need: privacy protection is often free; "SEO tools" bundles rarely help.
  • Auto-renewal turned off, letting the domain expire. An expired domain takes your website and email offline, and can be bought by someone else.

Keep everything in your name

Register the domain and hosting accounts with your own email, in your own or your company's name, and save the logins somewhere safe. If a designer buys them for you, ask for the accounts to be transferred to you at launch.

A typical yearly running cost

For a small business website: about ₹1,000 for the domain plus ₹2,000–₹8,000 for good hosting, so roughly ₹3,000–₹9,000 a year. Add business email if you need it. For everything else that goes into running a site, see our guide to website maintenance costs.
