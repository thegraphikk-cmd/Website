Google now shows AI-generated answers, called AI Overviews, above the usual results for many searches, and more people ask questions in AI assistants like ChatGPT, Gemini and others. Small business owners reasonably ask: will anyone still visit my website? The answer is yes, but the way you earn those visits is shifting.

What's changing

  • Simple questions ("what is a domain name?") are often answered directly on the results page, so fewer people click through.
  • AI answers cite sources. Pages that explain things clearly and accurately are more likely to be referenced.
  • Local and buying searches ("dentist near me", "website designer in Chennai") still lead heavily to Maps listings, reviews and business websites.

What stays the same

AI systems summarise information from the web. They still need trustworthy, specific, up-to-date sources. The fundamentals of good SEO, helpful content, a clear site and a real reputation, matter as much as ever.

Practical steps

1. Answer questions clearly and early

Put a direct, short answer near the top of a page, then the detail. Use clear headings for each question. This helps readers, Google's normal results and AI summaries alike.

2. Show real experience

Generic content is easy for AI to replace. Content with your own prices, local details, project examples, photos and opinions is not. Mention who wrote it and why they know the topic.

3. Keep facts accurate and current

Update prices, rules and yearly guides. Outdated information is less likely to be trusted or cited.

4. Strengthen local signals

For local businesses, your Google Business Profile, reviews and consistent listings feed directly into local results and AI answers about businesses nearby.

5. Use structured data

Schema markup helps search engines understand your business details, products and articles precisely. See schema markup in plain English.

6. Make your site easy to read for machines and people

  • Fast pages with real text, not text inside images.
  • A sensible structure with headings, lists and tables.
  • Pages that Google can crawl; check Search Console for indexing problems.

Focus on searches that lead to action

"What is SEO?" may increasingly be answered without a click. "SEO agency for dental clinics in Chennai" or "website cost for a 10-page business site" still lead people to compare providers. Make sure your service pages, pricing information and case studies are excellent. These are the pages that turn searches into customers.

Build a brand people search for

When people search your business name directly, you win regardless of AI. Reviews, word of mouth, social media and memorable service all build that.

Measure what matters

Track enquiries and sales, not just traffic. You might see fewer clicks on general articles while enquiries from service pages hold steady or grow. That's a good trade. See website analytics basics.
