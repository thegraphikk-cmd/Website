Travellers compare tour packages carefully: itinerary, hotels, inclusions, price and whether the agency is trustworthy. Big booking sites set high expectations for information and ease. A small travel agency can compete by being clearer, more personal and more responsive.

Package pages that answer everything

Each package or tour deserves its own page with:

  • Destination and duration: "Kerala Backwaters & Munnar – 5 Nights / 6 Days".
  • Day-by-day itinerary with places, activities and travel times.
  • Hotels: names or categories, room type and meal plan.
  • Inclusions and exclusions, listed clearly (transfers, sightseeing, entry tickets, GST, flights).
  • Price per person, with the basis (twin sharing, season), plus "starting from".
  • Photos of the destination and, ideally, real trips you've run.
  • FAQs: best time to visit, what to pack, visa needs.
  • Cancellation policy summary.

Organise packages the way travellers think

Group them by destination, type (honeymoon, family, pilgrimage, adventure, group tours), duration and budget. Filters help visitors find the right trip quickly.

Build trust

  • Registrations and memberships, where applicable (for example Ministry of Tourism approval, IATA, or associations like TAAI or IATO).
  • Office address and photos of your team.
  • Reviews and photos from past travellers; see how to get more Google reviews.
  • Clear payment and refund terms, including GST and TCS where they apply to overseas packages.
  • A real phone number and WhatsApp.

Make enquiries easy

  • An enquiry form on every package: travel dates, number of travellers, departure city, budget.
  • A WhatsApp button with the package name pre-filled.
  • A "Customise this trip" option. Personal itineraries are where small agencies shine.
  • A quick response, ideally the same day with a draft itinerary.

Online payments

Accept booking amounts and balance payments online through UPI, cards and netbanking. Payment links work well for custom itineraries; see setting up payments.

Content that brings travellers

Travel is one of the most researched purchases. Helpful guides attract visitors months before they book:

  • "Best time to visit Ladakh"
  • "Kerala itinerary for 5 days"
  • "Visa for Dubai for Indians: documents and process"
  • "Budget for a Bali honeymoon from Chennai"

Link each guide to the matching package. See does a small business need a blog?

Photos and speed

Travel sites love big images, which often makes them slow. Compress photos, avoid heavy sliders and auto-playing videos, and test on a phone; see image SEO basics.

Be found locally

Many travellers still prefer an agency in their own city. A strong Google Business Profile, local reviews and pages like "Tour packages from Chennai" help you show up for them.
