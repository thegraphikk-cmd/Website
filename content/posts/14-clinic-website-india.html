Patients choose clinics the same way they choose everything else now: they search on Google, compare a few options on the map, and check the website before calling. A clear clinic website answers their questions, builds trust and makes booking easy. Here's what to include, plus what to keep out.

What patients look for first

When someone lands on a clinic website, they're usually trying to answer four questions quickly:

  1. Does this clinic treat my problem?
  2. Is it near me, and when is it open?
  3. Who is the doctor, and are they qualified?
  4. How do I book?

Your home page should answer all four without scrolling far.

Essential pages

Home

Clinic name, specialty, location, timings and a prominent Book appointment button (WhatsApp or call). Add a short line on what makes the clinic different, such as specific expertise, languages spoken, or parking.

Doctor profiles

Photo, qualifications, registration details, experience and areas of focus. Patients want to know who they will see. Real photos build far more trust than stock images.

Treatments or services

A page for each main treatment or condition, explained in plain language: what it is, who it's for, what to expect, and how long it takes. These pages are also what helps you appear when people search for specific treatments in your area.

Timings, location and contact

Consulting hours for each doctor, holiday timings, a Google Map, parking information and directions from known landmarks.

Patient information

What to bring, how to prepare, payment methods and insurance or cashless options if you offer them.

Booking: keep it simple

For most clinics, a WhatsApp booking button with a pre-filled message ("Hi, I'd like to book an appointment with Dr. ___") works better than a complex online booking system. Patients are comfortable with WhatsApp, and your front desk can confirm slots. Larger clinics with several doctors may benefit from a proper booking system linked to their schedule.

Local SEO for clinics

  • A complete, verified Google Business Profile with the right category, such as "Dermatologist" or "Dental clinic"
  • Clinic name, address and phone exactly the same on the website and profile
  • Page titles like "Dermatologist in Adyar, Chennai"
  • Patients encouraged to leave genuine Google reviews

What to avoid

Medical practitioners in India are subject to professional conduct and advertising rules from the National Medical Commission and state medical councils, and these have changed over time. Check the current rules that apply to you before publishing.

As general good practice:

  • Keep it factual. Describe services, qualifications and facilities, not superlatives like "best doctor in Chennai".
  • Avoid promising results or guaranteed cures.
  • Be careful with before-and-after photos and testimonials; check what is permitted and get written patient consent for anything you publish.
  • Protect patient privacy. Never show identifiable patient information without consent, and keep any forms that collect health information secure.
  • Don't publish discounts or offers that could be seen as soliciting patients, unless you've confirmed they're allowed.

Design tips for clinic websites

  • Calm, clean design with plenty of white space and readable text
  • Large tap targets for older patients on phones
  • Tamil and English content if your patients prefer it
  • Fast loading, because many patients search on mobile data
