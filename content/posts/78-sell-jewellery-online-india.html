Jewellery is a high-trust purchase. Customers worry about purity, authenticity, sizing and safe delivery, and they can't hold the piece before buying. Whether you sell fine gold jewellery, silver or fashion jewellery, your website has to answer those worries clearly.

Lead with trust

  • Your story and physical presence: years in business, showroom address and photos.
  • Certifications: BIS hallmarking for gold, and diamond or gemstone certificates where applicable.
  • Reviews and customer photos.
  • Clear policies: returns, exchange, buyback and lifetime exchange if you offer it.
  • Real contact: phone, WhatsApp and video-call consultations.

Hallmarking and purity details

BIS hallmarking with a HUID (hallmark unique identification) is mandatory for most gold jewellery sold in India, with some exceptions. Mention clearly that your gold jewellery is BIS hallmarked, state the purity (22K/916, 18K/750, 14K) and explain how customers can verify the HUID, for example through the BIS CARE app. Check the current rules for your products.

Product pages for jewellery

  • Close-up photos from several angles on a clean background.
  • On-model photos to show scale.
  • Short videos showing sparkle and movement. They make a big difference.
  • Weights: gross weight, net metal weight and stone weight.
  • Dimensions and ring or bangle size guides.
  • Price breakdown: metal value, making charges, stone value and GST.

Pricing gold and silver

Metal rates change daily. Options include updating prices daily from the current rate, showing "price on request" for high-value pieces, or offering a booking amount with final billing at the day's rate. Whatever you choose, be transparent about how the price is calculated.

Payments

  • UPI, cards, netbanking and EMI options for higher-value pieces.
  • Check your payment gateway's limits and rules for jewellery and high-value transactions.
  • For COD, consider limits or advance payment on expensive items to avoid risky refused deliveries.

Safe, insured shipping

  • Use couriers or services that handle valuables with insurance.
  • Tamper-evident packaging, plus a packing video for high-value orders.
  • OTP-based delivery where available.
  • Clear delivery timelines and tracking.

Help customers choose

  • Video calls to show pieces live.
  • Try-at-home or showroom pickup options, if feasible.
  • Collections by occasion: bridal, daily wear, gifting, festive.

Be found

Many customers still research online and buy in-store. A strong Google Business Profile, reviews and a website showing your collections bring both online orders and showroom visits. See marketplaces vs your own website for the bigger picture.
