"Should we have a blog?" is one of the most common questions small business owners ask. The honest answer: a blog is worth it if you'll publish helpful articles regularly that answer your customers' questions. A blog with three posts from 2022 does more harm than having none.

How a blog brings customers

Your service pages rank for "service + location" searches. But before people search for a provider, they often search questions:

  • "How much does a modular kitchen cost?"
  • "Is it better to repair or replace an AC?"
  • "What documents are needed for GST registration?"

A helpful article that answers these questions gets found, builds trust, and leads readers to your service. Over time, a library of good articles brings steady visitors without paying for ads.

When a blog makes sense

  • Customers research before buying (most services, higher-priced products).
  • You get the same questions again and again.
  • You can publish at least two to four useful posts a month, yourself or with help.
  • You're in it for the long term: blogs take months to build traffic.

When it might not

  • Customers decide on impulse or purely on location ("tea shop near me").
  • Nobody has time to write, and there's no budget for help.

In those cases, focus on your Google Business Profile, reviews and service pages first. See SEO for a new website.

What to write about

  • Cost questions: "How much does X cost in [city]?" These are some of the most searched topics.
  • Comparisons: "X vs Y: which is right for you?"
  • How-to guides related to your service.
  • Checklists: "What to prepare before…"
  • Mistakes to avoid and signs you need your service.
  • Local guides relevant to your customers.

Use keyword research and your Search Console data to find topics people actually search.

How to write posts that work

  • Answer the question early. Give the short answer near the top, then the detail.
  • Be specific and honest: real prices, real examples, real experience.
  • Use clear headings so readers can skim.
  • Link to your services and related posts; see internal linking.
  • End with a next step: a quote, a call or a WhatsApp message.

How often to post

Consistency beats volume. Two good posts a month for a year will do more than twenty rushed posts in one month followed by silence. Scheduling posts in advance helps keep the rhythm.

What about AI-written content?

AI tools can help with outlines and drafts, but mass-produced generic articles rarely help. Google rewards content that's genuinely helpful and shows real experience. Add your own knowledge, local details, prices and examples, and check facts carefully. See AI search and small businesses.

Measure it

Watch impressions and clicks for blog posts in Search Console, and track enquiries that start on blog pages. Expect results to build gradually over three to twelve months.
