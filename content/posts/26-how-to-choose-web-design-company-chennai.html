Search "web design company in Chennai" and you'll find hundreds of options, each claiming to be the best. Ratings and big claims don't tell you much. What does help is checking a few specific things and asking the right questions. This guide gives you a simple process.

Step 1: Shortlist from real work, not ads

Start with three to five companies whose live websites you like. Open the sites they built on your phone, not just screenshots in their portfolio:

  • Do they load quickly on mobile data?
  • Is it easy to find prices, services and contact details?
  • Do they look different from each other, or like the same template recoloured?
  • Have they built sites for businesses like yours?

Step 2: Check reviews properly

Read individual Google reviews rather than the average rating. Look for reviews that mention specific projects, timelines and support after launch. A handful of detailed reviews is worth more than many one-line ones.

Step 3: Ask these 12 questions

About the work

  1. Can you show me three live sites similar to what I need?
  2. Who will actually design and build my website?
  3. Will the design be custom or based on a template? Either can be fine; you just need to know.
  4. How will you make the site fast on mobile?
  5. What do you do for SEO as part of the build?

About price and scope

  1. Is the price fixed? What exactly is included?
  2. How many rounds of revisions are included?
  3. What will cost extra later: content changes, new pages, hosting?
  4. What's the payment schedule?

About ownership and support

  1. Will the domain, hosting and website files be in my name?
  2. Can I edit text and photos myself? Is training included?
  3. What support do I get after launch, for how long, and at what cost?

Red flags

  • Guaranteed Google rankings. Nobody can guarantee rankings.
  • Very vague quotes, like "Website – ₹50,000" with no breakdown.
  • Domain registered in their name, or reluctance to share logins.
  • No live examples, only mockups.
  • Pressure to decide today with a "discount".
  • Slow, unclear replies before you've paid. Communication rarely improves after.

Step 4: Compare quotes fairly

Put the quotes side by side and check they cover the same things: number of pages, custom vs template design, content writing, SEO setup, revisions, support period, and hosting. A cheaper quote often leaves out items you'll pay for later. Our Chennai website cost guide shows what's typical at each price level.

Step 5: Trust the conversation

The best sign of a good web design partner is that they ask about your business before talking about design: your customers, your goals, how people find you today. If the first call is all about colours and animations, keep looking.
