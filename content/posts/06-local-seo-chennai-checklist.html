Local SEO is how a business shows up when someone nearby searches for what it sells: "AC service in Tambaram", "dentist near me", "best biryani in Anna Nagar". For most Chennai businesses, these searches bring the highest-intent customers on the internet. This checklist covers what actually moves rankings, in the order we'd tackle it.

How Google ranks local businesses

Google says local results are based mainly on three things:

  • Relevance: how well your profile and website match the search.
  • Distance: how far you are from the person searching or the area they named.
  • Prominence: how well known and trusted you are, judged from reviews, links, mentions and your website.

You can't move your shop closer to every customer, but you can improve relevance and prominence a lot.

1. Google Business Profile (the biggest lever)

  • ☐ Profile created, verified and owned by a business account you control
  • ☐ Business name exactly as on your signboard, with no keyword stuffing
  • ☐ Most specific primary category, plus relevant secondary categories
  • ☐ Address shown if customers visit, or service areas if you travel to them
  • ☐ Hours, holiday hours, phone and website filled in
  • ☐ Every service listed with a short description
  • ☐ At least 10 real photos: outside, inside, team and work
  • ☐ A post or photo update at least every couple of weeks

If you haven't set this up yet, start with our guide to adding your business to Google Maps.

2. Reviews

  • ☐ A short review link saved and shared with every happy customer on WhatsApp
  • ☐ A steady flow of new reviews, not a burst once a year
  • ☐ Replies to every review, positive or negative, within a few days
  • ☐ Customers mentioning the service and area naturally (don't script them)

Never buy reviews or post fake ones. Google filters them and can suspend the profile.

3. Consistent name, address and phone (NAP)

Google cross-checks your details across the web. Mismatched phone numbers or old addresses weaken trust.

  • ☐ Same name, address and phone on your website, Google profile and social pages
  • ☐ Listings checked and corrected on Justdial, Sulekha, IndiaMART and industry directories you appear on
  • ☐ Old addresses and disconnected numbers updated everywhere

4. Your website

Your website is where Google learns what you do in detail. A Google profile with no website, or a slow one, usually ranks below competitors with a good site.

  • ☐ Each main service has its own page (one "Services" page listing everything is weaker)
  • ☐ Page titles include the service and area, for example "Interior Designer in Velachery, Chennai"
  • ☐ Name, address and phone in the footer of every page
  • ☐ Contact page with an embedded Google Map
  • ☐ LocalBusiness schema markup with your hours and location
  • ☐ Fast on mobile: under about 2.5 seconds to show the main content
  • ☐ Clear click-to-call and WhatsApp buttons

5. Location pages (only if they're genuinely useful)

If you serve several parts of Chennai, a page per area can help, but only if each page has real local content: projects you did there, travel time, area-specific pricing or FAQs. Pages that just swap the area name are thin content and can hurt more than help.

6. Local links and mentions

  • ☐ Listed with your local trade association or chamber of commerce
  • ☐ Mentioned by suppliers, partners or brands you stock
  • ☐ Featured in local news, event sponsorships or community pages
  • ☐ Guest posts or interviews on Chennai blogs and Instagram pages relevant to your field

A handful of genuine local links beats hundreds of directory submissions.

7. Content that answers local questions

Blog posts and FAQ pages that answer what Chennai customers ask ("How much does a modular kitchen cost in Chennai?", "Which areas do you deliver to?") give Google more reasons to show you, and give customers reasons to trust you.

8. Track the results

  • ☐ Google Search Console set up to see which searches bring visitors
  • ☐ Google Business Profile performance checked monthly: calls, direction requests, website clicks
  • ☐ A simple note of where new customers say they found you

How long does local SEO take?

Profile improvements and new reviews can show results within weeks. Website and content improvements usually take two to six months to show up fully in rankings. The businesses that win locally are rarely the ones that did one big push. They keep adding reviews, photos and useful pages month after month.
