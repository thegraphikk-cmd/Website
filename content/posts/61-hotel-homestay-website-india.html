Booking sites like MakeMyTrip, Booking.com, Agoda and Airbnb bring guests, but they typically take a commission of roughly 15% to 25% on every stay. For a small hotel or homestay, that can be the difference between a good season and a break-even one. Your own website won't replace the platforms, but every booking you move to it keeps that commission in your pocket.

Why guests book on the platforms

  • They found you there and it feels safe.
  • Booking is quick, with instant confirmation.
  • Your own website was hard to find, slow, or had no way to book.

Many guests actually check the property's own website before booking. If it looks good, shows prices and lets them book easily, a good share will book direct.

What your website needs

1. A booking engine

A real "Check availability" button with dates, room types and online payment. Hotel booking engines (often bundled with a channel manager) sync availability with the OTAs, so you don't get double bookings. For a small homestay, a simple enquiry form plus WhatsApp can work to start.

2. Great photos

Photos sell rooms. Show every room type, bathrooms, views, common areas, food and the surroundings, in good daylight. Invest in a professional shoot; it pays back quickly. Compress images so pages stay fast on mobile; see testing your website speed.

3. Clear room pages

For each room: size, beds, maximum guests, amenities, view, and price "from". Guests compare carefully, so missing details push them back to the OTA listing.

4. Location and things to do

Distance from the airport, railway station and attractions, with a map. A short local guide ("10 things to do near our homestay in Kodaikanal") helps guests and Google.

5. Reviews

Show your Google and platform ratings. Guests trust them more than your own description. See how to get more Google reviews.

Give guests a reason to book direct

  • Best rate on our website, or a small direct-booking discount, where your OTA contracts allow it.
  • Extras: free breakfast, early check-in, airport pickup or a welcome drink.
  • Flexible cancellation for direct bookings.
  • A real person on WhatsApp to answer questions; see adding a WhatsApp button.

Get found on Google

  • Google Business Profile with photos, amenities and your website link. See adding your business to Google Maps.
  • Google's free hotel booking links: Google can show your official site as a booking option alongside OTAs when your booking engine or channel manager supports it. Ask your provider.
  • Clear page titles: "Homestay in Coorg with Valley View | Book Direct".

Turn past guests into repeat guests

OTA guests often belong to the platform. Direct guests belong to you. Collect emails and WhatsApp numbers (with permission), send a thank-you after the stay, and share offers for festivals and long weekends.

Keep the OTAs, but balance them

For most small properties the platforms remain important for discovery. The goal isn't to leave them, but to shift a growing share of bookings, especially repeat and returning guests, to your own website.
