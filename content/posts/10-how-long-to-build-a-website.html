"Can we go live next week?" is one of the most common questions we hear. Sometimes the answer is yes. Usually, a good website takes a few weeks, and the biggest factor isn't design or development. It's how quickly content and feedback arrive.

Typical timelines

ProjectTypical timeline
Landing page1–2 weeks
Portfolio website1–3 weeks
Small business site (up to ~6 pages)2–3 weeks
Custom business site with blog (10–15 pages)4–6 weeks
Online store (up to ~50 products)4–5 weeks
Larger store (hundreds of products)6–8 weeks
Redesign of an existing site4–6 weeks
Web app or portal10+ weeks

These assume content is ready on time and feedback comes back within a couple of days.

What happens during a typical 4-week build

Week 1: Discovery and structure

Goals, audience and competitors are agreed. The page list and sitemap are finalised, and content is collected.

Week 2: Design

The home page is designed first, then the main inner pages. You review and request changes.

Week 3: Build

The approved design is built, content is added, and forms, WhatsApp, maps and other features are connected.

Week 4: Test and launch

The site is tested on phones and desktop, checked for speed and SEO basics, connected to Google Search Console and launched on your domain.

What causes delays

  • Content not ready. Waiting for service descriptions, photos or the About page is the number-one cause of delay.
  • Slow or conflicting feedback. Several people giving different opinions, or feedback arriving a week late.
  • Scope creep. New pages or features added halfway through.
  • Domain and hosting access. Nobody remembers the login for the domain bought five years ago.
  • Third parties. Payment gateway approval, logo designers or photographers.

How to launch faster

  1. Prepare content before the project starts. Our pre-project checklist lists everything.
  2. Choose one person to give feedback and approve.
  3. Agree on the page list and features upfront, and save extras for phase two.
  4. Sort out domain and hosting logins early.
  5. For online stores, apply for your payment gateway at the start, since approval can take time.

Can a website be built in a few days?

Yes, if it's a simple landing page or template site and all the content is ready. But a site built in two days usually skips discovery, testing and SEO setup. If the site needs to rank on Google and convert visitors, a few weeks is time well spent.
