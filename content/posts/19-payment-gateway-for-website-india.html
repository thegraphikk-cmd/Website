To take online payments in India you need a payment gateway, a service that securely collects money by UPI, cards, netbanking or wallets and settles it to your bank account. Several good gateways serve Indian businesses. The right one depends on your business type, your platform and the fees for the payment methods your customers use most.

Popular payment gateways in India

Commonly used options include Razorpay, PayU, Cashfree, PhonePe Payment Gateway, CCAvenue and Paytm. All support UPI, major cards and netbanking. Most work with Shopify, WooCommerce and custom websites.

Gateway fees, offers and supported features change regularly, so compare current pricing pages before choosing.

Documents you'll usually need for KYC

  • PAN of the business (or proprietor)
  • Business proof: GST certificate, shop and establishment licence, or company documents
  • Bank account details with a cancelled cheque or statement
  • Address proof and ID of the owner or directors
  • A live website with clear contact details, pricing, and policies (terms, privacy, refund and cancellation, shipping)

That last point catches many businesses out: gateways often review your website before approval, so publish your policy pages before applying.

UPI, cards or COD?

UPI

The most popular way Indians pay online. Fast, familiar, and often cheaper than cards. Make it prominent at checkout.

Cards and netbanking

Important for higher-value orders and customers who prefer credit cards for rewards or EMI. Usually a higher fee than UPI.

Cash on delivery

Still expected for many product categories and new customers, but expensive when orders are refused. We explain how to manage it in our guide to COD on Shopify.

Setting it up

Shopify

Install your gateway's official app or select it under Settings → Payments, connect your approved merchant account, and place a live test order. Check whether your Shopify plan charges an additional transaction fee when using a third-party gateway.

WooCommerce

Install the gateway's official plugin, enter your API keys from the gateway dashboard, and test in the gateway's test mode before switching to live keys.

Custom websites and service businesses

Service businesses that don't need a full store can use payment links or payment pages from the gateway, shared on WhatsApp or embedded as a "Pay now" button on the website.

Before going live

  • ☐ Test a successful payment, a failed payment and a refund
  • ☐ Check the order and invoice are created correctly
  • ☐ Confirm settlement reaches your bank account
  • ☐ Turn on payment notifications for your team
