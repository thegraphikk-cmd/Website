When parents shortlist schools, the website is usually the first place they look, often late at night on a phone. A clear, up-to-date site answers their questions, builds trust and fills your admission enquiry list. An outdated one, with last year's circulars and a broken gallery, quietly sends them elsewhere.

What parents look for first

From the questions schools receive, parents want quick answers to:

  • Board and classes: CBSE, ICSE, state board or IB, and which grades.
  • Admissions: dates, age criteria, process and documents needed.
  • Fees: at least a range, or a clear way to ask.
  • Location and transport: bus routes and areas covered.
  • Safety: CCTV, staff verification, medical room, campus security.
  • Teachers and results: qualifications, board results, achievements.
  • Real photos: classrooms, labs, library, playground and events.

If these take more than a tap or two to find, parents move on.

Pages every school website needs

  1. Home: board, classes, admissions open/closed, a clear "Enquire now" button and contact numbers.
  2. About: history, vision, management and principal's message (kept short).
  3. Admissions: process, eligibility, important dates, documents, and an online enquiry form.
  4. Academics: curriculum, subjects, teaching approach, co-curricular activities.
  5. Facilities: labs, library, sports, transport, with photos.
  6. Gallery and events: recent and dated, so parents see an active school.
  7. Notices and circulars: for current parents, easy for staff to update.
  8. Mandatory disclosures: where your board requires them.
  9. Contact: address with map, phone, email, office hours and WhatsApp.

Mandatory disclosures

Affiliated schools are often required to publish certain information on their website. CBSE, for example, has required a mandatory public disclosure section with details such as affiliation, documents, results, staff and infrastructure. Requirements change, so check your board's latest circular and keep this section current. It's also one of the first things some parents check.

Make admissions easy

  • Put the enquiry form on the home page and admissions page: name, class applying for, phone, area.
  • Add a WhatsApp button for quick questions; see how to add a WhatsApp button.
  • Offer a campus visit booking, even if it's just a form with preferred dates.
  • Send enquiries to an email or sheet someone checks daily during admission season.

Keep it easy to update

The biggest problem with school websites isn't design. It's that nobody updates them. Before building, decide:

  • Who updates notices, events and the gallery.
  • How: a simple admin panel that office staff can use without a developer.
  • How often: at least monthly, and weekly during admissions.

A site with fresh notices and recent event photos signals an active, well-run school.

Photos: real, recent and light

Use real photos of your campus and students (with consent), not stock images. Parents can tell. Compress images before uploading, because a gallery of 5 MB photos makes the site painfully slow on mobile data. Avoid showing children's full names alongside their photos.

Be found on Google

  • Set up and verify your Google Business Profile with photos and the right category.
  • Ask parents for Google reviews.
  • Use clear page titles like "CBSE School in Velachery, Chennai | Admissions 2027".
  • Keep the site fast on mobile; most parents browse on phones.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Last year's admission dates still on the home page.
  • Important information only in PDF circulars that are hard to read on a phone.
  • Auto-playing videos and slow image sliders.
  • No phone number or a number nobody answers.
