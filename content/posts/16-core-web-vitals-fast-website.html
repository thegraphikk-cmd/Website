Most visitors to Indian business websites arrive on a phone, often on mobile data that isn't as fast as the advertising suggests. If your site takes five seconds to show anything, many of them leave before they see what you offer. Google measures this experience with Core Web Vitals and uses it as one of many ranking signals. Here's what they mean and how to improve them.

The three Core Web Vitals

MetricWhat it measuresGood score
LCP – Largest Contentful PaintHow long until the main content (usually the big image or headline) appears2.5 seconds or less
INP – Interaction to Next PaintHow quickly the page responds when you tap or click200 milliseconds or less
CLS – Cumulative Layout ShiftHow much the page jumps around while loading0.1 or less

Google looks at how real visitors experience your site over time, so a fast test on your office Wi-Fi doesn't tell the whole story.

How to check your site

  • PageSpeed Insights (pagespeed.web.dev): enter your URL to see real-user data, if Google has enough, plus a lab test with specific suggestions.
  • Google Search Console: the Core Web Vitals report groups your pages into good, needs improvement and poor.
  • Your own phone on mobile data: the simplest test of all. Open your site with Wi-Fi off.

Why most small business sites are slow

  1. Huge images. A 4 MB photo straight from a phone camera, displayed at 400 pixels wide.
  2. Heavy themes and page builders that load code for features you don't use.
  3. Too many plugins or apps, each adding its own scripts.
  4. Cheap, overloaded hosting that takes a second or more just to start responding.
  5. Sliders, videos and animations at the top of the page.
  6. Third-party scripts: chat widgets, multiple tracking pixels, embedded social feeds.

Fixes with the biggest impact

Improve LCP (loading)

  • Compress images and serve modern formats like WebP or AVIF at the size they're displayed.
  • Make the main hero image load first, and don't lazy-load it.
  • Replace image sliders at the top of the page with a single strong image.
  • Use good hosting with caching and a CDN.

Improve INP (responsiveness)

  • Remove plugins, apps and scripts you don't need.
  • Load chat widgets and non-essential scripts after the page is usable.
  • Avoid heavy page-builder effects and animations on mobile.

Improve CLS (stability)

  • Give images and embeds fixed dimensions so space is reserved before they load.
  • Don't push content down with late-loading banners or pop-ups.
  • Load fonts in a way that doesn't make text jump.

Does speed really affect sales?

Speed is one of many ranking factors; great content on a slightly slower site can still outrank a fast site with thin content. But speed directly affects how many visitors stay long enough to call, message or buy. For ads, it matters even more: you pay for every click, including the people who leave while your page loads.

Quick wins you can do this week

  • Compress and resize every image over 300 KB.
  • Delete plugins and apps you no longer use.
  • Remove auto-playing videos and sliders from the home page.
  • Check your site on your phone with mobile data, and fix whatever annoys you.
