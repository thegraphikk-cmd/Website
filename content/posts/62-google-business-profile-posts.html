Setting up a Google Business Profile is step one. Keeping it active is what makes it work. Profiles with fresh photos, recent posts and complete information look more trustworthy to customers, and give Google more reasons to show you. Here's how to keep yours alive in about 30 minutes a month.

1. Posts: updates, offers and events

From your profile (search your business name on Google while logged in, or use the Business Profile Manager), choose Add update. You can post:

  • Updates: news, new services, tips, a recent project.
  • Offers: a discount or deal with start and end dates.
  • Events: workshops, launches, open days.

Add a real photo, keep the text short, and include a button like "Learn more" or "Call now" that links to the right page on your website. One or two posts a month is enough for most businesses.

2. Photos: the most viewed part of your profile

  • Logo and cover photo so your profile looks official.
  • Exterior photos that help people recognise your shop or office from the street.
  • Interior, team and work in progress.
  • Products or finished work: dishes, rooms, haircuts, projects.

Add a few new photos every month. Customers can add photos too, so yours should be the best ones people see.

3. Products and services

List your main services and products with short descriptions and, where possible, prices. This helps your profile appear for more specific searches and saves customers a call.

4. Questions and answers

Customers may ask questions publicly on your profile, depending on how Google shows this feature in your area, and anyone can answer, sometimes wrongly. Check regularly and reply quickly. You can also think about the questions you hear most and make sure your profile description, services and website answer them clearly.

5. Keep the basics accurate

  • Hours, including special hours for festivals and holidays.
  • Phone and website, pointing to the right page.
  • Attributes: wheelchair access, women-owned, online appointments and so on.
  • Description: what you do, who you serve and where, in plain words without keyword stuffing.

Avoid changing your business name, address or category often; frequent changes can trigger a review. See why profiles get suspended.

6. Reviews and replies

Reply to every review, positive or negative. It shows you're active and care. See how to get more Google reviews.

A 30-minute monthly routine

  1. Add one or two posts with photos.
  2. Upload three to five new photos.
  3. Reply to new reviews and questions.
  4. Check hours, phone and services are correct.
  5. Look at the Performance section: calls, direction requests and website clicks.
