Parents and students choosing a coaching centre, whether for NEET, JEE, school tuition, spoken English or skill courses, research carefully. They compare results, faculty, batch timings and fees across several institutes, mostly on phones. A clear website helps you stand out from centres that only have a phone number on Justdial.

What parents and students look for

  • Courses offered, for which classes or exams.
  • Batch timings and mode: offline, online or hybrid.
  • Faculty: qualifications and experience.
  • Results: past performance, shown honestly.
  • Fees and payment options, or at least a range.
  • Location, safety and facilities.
  • A demo class or counselling session.

Pages to include

  1. Home: who you teach, main courses, a results highlight, and a "Book a free demo class" button.
  2. A page per course: syllabus coverage, duration, batch sizes, timings, study material, tests, fees and FAQs.
  3. Faculty: photos, subjects, qualifications and years of teaching.
  4. Results: student achievements with consent.
  5. About and facilities: classrooms, library, test series, doubt sessions.
  6. Contact: map, phone, WhatsApp, office hours.

Show results honestly

Results are your strongest proof, but exaggerated claims damage trust and can attract regulatory scrutiny. Coaching advertising in India has drawn attention for misleading claims, and guidelines on coaching centres have been issued in recent years. Use real names and photos only with written consent, state the exam and year, and don't imply guaranteed ranks or selections. Check current rules that apply to your type of institute.

Make enquiries easy

  • A short form: student name, class or exam, phone, preferred batch.
  • A WhatsApp button with a pre-filled message naming the course.
  • Demo-class booking with available dates.
  • Quick follow-up. During admission season, the institute that calls back first often wins.

Online classes and payments

If you teach online, show the platform you use, recorded-class access and test portals. Accepting fees online through UPI or cards makes enrolment easier; see setting up payments. For monthly fees, recurring payments can help; see subscriptions and recurring payments.

Be found locally

  • Set up your Google Business Profile with photos and the right category.
  • Use clear titles: "NEET Coaching in Tambaram | Small Batches".
  • Ask parents and students for Google reviews.
  • If you have branches, give each its own page; see ranking in more than one area.

Helpful content

Exam timelines, preparation tips, syllabus breakdowns and "how to choose" guides attract students searching on Google and show your expertise. Keep them accurate and updated each year.
