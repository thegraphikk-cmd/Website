"Dentist near me", "AC service near me", "cake shop near me open now". These searches are some of the most valuable a local business can appear for, because the person usually wants to buy soon. But ranking for them doesn't work the way many people think.

How "near me" searches work

Google uses the searcher's location to find businesses nearby, mostly through Google Maps and the local results at the top of the page. The words "near me" are mainly a signal of local intent. Google doesn't look for pages that literally say "near me".

That's why stuffing "best plumber near me" into your website doesn't help. What helps is giving Google strong signals that you're a relevant, trustworthy business in the searcher's area.

The three local ranking factors

  • Relevance: does your business match what they searched?
  • Distance: how close are you to the searcher?
  • Prominence: how well known and well reviewed are you?

You can't move closer to every customer, but you can maximise relevance and prominence.

1. Complete your Google Business Profile

  • Primary category: the most specific one that fits ("Pediatric dentist" rather than "Dentist" if that's your speciality).
  • Additional categories for other main services.
  • Accurate hours, including "open now" accuracy for holidays.
  • Services, products, photos and a clear description.

Start with adding your business to Google Maps and keep it active with regular posts and photos.

2. Get steady reviews

The number, recency and rating of reviews all affect how prominent you are. Reviews that mention your services and area also help relevance. See how to get more reviews.

3. Make your website support your profile

  • Your name, address and phone exactly matching your profile.
  • Service pages describing what you do, in the words customers use.
  • Your area mentioned naturally: "AC servicing in Adyar, Besant Nagar and Thiruvanmiyur".
  • An embedded map and directions on the contact page.
  • Fast, mobile-friendly pages. Most "near me" searches happen on phones.

4. Consistent listings elsewhere

Appearing with the same details on Justdial, Bing Places, Apple Maps and industry directories reinforces your location and legitimacy. See free business listings.

5. Be open when people search

Searches like "open now" filter out closed businesses. Accurate hours, and longer hours if your business can offer them, can make a real difference.

Service-area businesses

If you travel to customers and don't have a shopfront, set service areas in your profile and hide your address. You'll mostly appear for searches near your base, so build area pages for the main places you serve; see service area pages.
