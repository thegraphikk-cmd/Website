When someone in your area searches for what you do, Google shows a map with three businesses before any websites. Getting into that map is free, and for local businesses it is often the single biggest source of calls. Here is how to do it properly, from creating the profile to getting verified.

What you need before you start

  • A Google account you will keep long-term (ideally a business account, not a personal one you might lose access to).
  • Your exact business name, as it appears on your signboard, invoices or registration.
  • A phone number customers can call.
  • Your address, or the list of areas you serve if customers don't visit you.
  • Your website link, if you have one.

Step 1: Create the profile

  1. Go to business.google.com/create and sign in.
  2. Enter your business name. Use only your real name. Adding keywords like "Best Bakery Chennai" breaks Google's rules and is a common reason profiles get suspended.
  3. If Google suggests an existing listing with your name, claim it instead of creating a duplicate.

Step 2: Choose the right category

Your primary category is one of the strongest signals for which searches you appear in. Pick the most specific one that describes your main business: "Dentist" rather than "Doctor", "Bakery" rather than "Food". You can add more categories later for other services.

Step 3: Decide between an address and a service area

This is the step most people get wrong.

  • Customers visit you (shop, clinic, salon, restaurant): add your address. It will show on Maps.
  • You go to customers or work online (plumbers, caterers, designers, tutors who visit homes): choose not to show an address and add service areas instead. Google's rules say home-based businesses should hide their address.

For service areas, add the cities or districts you genuinely serve, roughly within a couple of hours' travel. Adding all of India won't help you rank and can get the profile flagged.

Step 4: Add contact details

Add your phone number and website. Make sure the name, address and phone number match exactly what's on your website. Mismatches confuse Google and weaken your ranking.

Step 5: Verify

Google needs proof that you run the business at that location. In India, verification is most commonly done by video recording, though some businesses get phone, SMS or email options. For a video, you typically need one continuous recording that shows:

  • Your location: street name, nearby landmarks, the building or signboard.
  • Your business in action: tools, equipment, stock or workspace.
  • Proof you manage it: unlocking the shop, opening the back office, or business documents.

Reviews can take a few business days. If it's rejected, Google tells you what was missing. Record a new video that fixes that point rather than repeating the same one.

Step 6: Complete the profile

A verified but empty profile won't rank well. Once verified, fill in:

  • Hours, including holidays.
  • Services or products with short descriptions and prices where possible.
  • Description (up to 750 characters) explaining what you do and where.
  • Photos: logo, cover, exterior, interior, team and work. Profiles with photos get noticeably more clicks.
  • Attributes such as wheelchair access, payment options or languages spoken.

Step 7: Get your first reviews

Reviews influence both ranking and whether people call you. In your profile, use Ask for reviews to get a short link, then send it to recent happy customers on WhatsApp. Reply to every review, good or bad. Never buy reviews or offer discounts in exchange for them; Google removes them and can penalise the profile.

Step 8: Connect it to your website

Your profile and website support each other. Put the same name, address and phone number on your website's contact page and footer, embed a Google Map, and link to your profile so customers can leave reviews. A website gives Google more information about what you do and where, which helps your profile rank.

How long until you show up?

After verification, a profile usually appears for searches of your business name within days. Ranking for general searches like "dentist near me" takes longer and depends on your category, distance from the searcher, reviews and how complete your profile is.
