It's one of the first decisions when starting a website: yourbusiness.com or yourbusiness.in? Many people believe one ranks better on Google. The truth is simpler, and the decision is mostly about your customers, not SEO.

Does .in rank better in India?

A .in domain is a country-code domain, and Google treats it as a signal that the site is aimed at India. A .com is generic: Google works out which country it serves from its content, language, addresses and links.

In practice, for a business serving Indian customers, both rank well. A .com with Indian content, prices in rupees, an Indian address and a Google Business Profile is clearly understood as Indian. Content quality, speed, reviews and links matter far more than the extension.

When .com is the better choice

  • You might sell or work outside India now or later.
  • The .com version of your name is available.
  • Your customers are likely to type .com by habit, which is true for most people.

When .in is the better choice

  • You only serve India, and want to signal that clearly.
  • The .com is taken but the .in is free.
  • You want a shorter or cleaner name that's only available as .in.

The best option: get both

If budget allows, register both and redirect one to the other. It protects your brand from copycats and catches people who type the wrong one. Both are inexpensive, roughly ₹500–₹1,500 a year each, depending on the registrar and offers.

What about .co.in, .net or newer extensions?

.co.in is fine but less familiar to customers. .net, .org (meant for non-profits), and newer ones like .store or .studio work technically, but people often mistype them or assume .com. Use them only if your preferred name is unavailable elsewhere.

How to choose a good domain name

  1. Short and easy to spell when you say it on the phone.
  2. Your brand name, not a string of keywords. "bestinteriordesignerchennai.com" looks spammy and limits you.
  3. No hyphens or numbers if you can avoid them.
  4. Check social handles are available with the same name.
  5. Check trademarks so you don't build a brand on someone else's name.

Register it in your own name

Whatever you choose, register the domain in your own or your company's name, with your own email, and keep the login safe. Many Indian businesses discover years later that their domain is registered to a former designer or employee. Recovering it can be slow and stressful.

Also check the renewal price, not just the first-year offer. Some registrars sell the first year cheaply and charge much more on renewal.

Already have a domain?

Changing domains later is possible but has a cost: you need redirects from every old page to keep your Google rankings. Unless your current domain is genuinely a problem, keep it and focus on improving the website.
