Business owners looking for a CA, a GST consultant or help with ITR filing usually start on Google. A clear, professional website helps them understand what you do and reach you. For chartered accountants, it also has to respect ICAI's rules on advertising and websites, which shape what you can and can't say.

ICAI's website guidelines, in brief

ICAI permits members and firms to have websites, within guidelines that broadly restrict solicitation and advertising-style claims. Typically, that means:

  • Sharing factual information: firm name, partners, qualifications, address, contact details and services offered.
  • Avoiding claims of superiority ("best CA in Chennai"), testimonials that solicit work, and promotional offers or discounts.
  • Taking care with how fees and client names are presented.

The guidelines are updated from time to time, so check the current ICAI guidelines (or ask your institute) before publishing, especially around client lists, testimonials and online advertising. Tax consultants and accountants who aren't ICAI members have more freedom, but the same plain, factual style still builds the most trust.

Pages to include

  1. Home: who you are, the services you offer, where you're based, and how to contact you.
  2. About the firm: partners, qualifications, years of practice and areas of expertise.
  3. Services: a page for each main area, such as audit, income tax, GST, company incorporation, accounting and payroll, and compliance.
  4. Industries served, if you specialise (for example, startups, exporters, doctors, real estate).
  5. Resources: due-date calendars, checklists and plain-English explainers.
  6. Contact: address with map, phone, email, office hours and an enquiry form.

Make service pages genuinely useful

Instead of a one-line list, explain each service: who needs it, what documents are required, the process and typical timelines. A page on "GST registration for small businesses" that answers real questions is helpful, factual and much more likely to be found on Google, without breaking any advertising rules.

Resources build trust

Helpful content is one of the best ways for professional firms to be found:

  • Compliance and due-date calendars for GST, TDS and income tax.
  • Document checklists for ITR filing, company registration or loans.
  • Short explainers on budget changes and new rules.

Keep them accurate and dated, and update them each year.

Make contact easy and secure

  • Click-to-call and a WhatsApp button for quick questions.
  • A short enquiry form: name, business type, service needed, phone.
  • Don't ask clients to upload PAN, Aadhaar or financial documents through a basic website form. Use a secure client portal or encrypted file-sharing instead.
  • A clear privacy policy explaining how you handle personal data.

Be found locally

  • Set up a Google Business Profile with the correct category, such as "Chartered accountant" or "Tax consultant".
  • Use clear page titles: "GST Registration and Filing in T. Nagar, Chennai".
  • Keep your firm's name, address and phone identical on your website, Google and other listings.

Design: calm and professional

Clients want to feel their finances are in safe hands. Use a clean layout, readable fonts, real photos of your team and office, and avoid flashy animations. Make sure it works well on mobile; business owners often look you up between meetings.
