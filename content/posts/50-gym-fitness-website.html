People looking for a gym, yoga studio or fitness class usually compare three or four places nearby before visiting one. Your website's job is simple: give them enough confidence to book a trial or walk in. Most fitness websites fail because they show slogans and stock photos instead of what people actually want to know.

What visitors want to know

  • Where you are, and how easy parking or transport is.
  • What you offer: gym floor, classes, personal training, yoga, Zumba, CrossFit.
  • When: opening hours and class timings.
  • How much: monthly, quarterly and yearly plans.
  • Who: trainers and their experience.
  • What it looks like inside, at the times they'd go.
  • Whether it suits them: beginners, women-only batches, seniors, kids.

Show your prices

"Contact us for pricing" makes people assume you're expensive and move on. Show your main plans, even as "starting from". You'll get fewer price-only calls and more serious visitors. If you run offers, put them clearly at the top with an end date.

Make the free trial the main button

A free or low-cost trial is the easiest yes. Make "Book a free trial" the main button on every page, with a short form: name, phone, preferred time and goal. Add a WhatsApp button too; many people prefer to ask a quick question first.

Class schedules people can read on a phone

Don't upload the schedule as a photo or PDF. Use a simple table or list by day that's easy to read on a phone and easy for your staff to update. Mark beginner-friendly classes and women-only batches clearly.

Real photos and trainers

  • Use real photos of your space, equipment and classes, not stock images of models.
  • Show your trainers with name, photo, certifications and specialities.
  • Short video tours work well; embed from YouTube or Instagram so the page stays fast.

Results and reviews

Member stories and transformations are powerful, with written consent. Keep them honest and avoid medical or guaranteed-result claims. Google reviews matter even more for local gyms; see how to get more reviews.

Be found nearby

  • Set up your Google Business Profile with photos, hours and the right categories (gym, yoga studio, fitness centre).
  • Use your area in page titles: "Gym in Velachery with Personal Training | Free Trial".
  • If you have several branches, give each one its own page and Business Profile; see ranking in more than one area.

Useful extras

  • Online membership payments and renewals via UPI.
  • Class booking for limited-capacity sessions.
  • A BMI or calorie calculator, or a simple diet guide, which helps with search traffic.

A simple structure

  1. Home: what you offer, offer or trial button, photos, reviews
  2. Memberships and pricing
  3. Classes and schedule
  4. Trainers
  5. Contact and trial booking

Keep it fast and clear, and update offers and schedules regularly. For budgets, see how much a website costs in India.
