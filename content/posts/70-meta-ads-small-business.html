Facebook and Instagram ads can bring enquiries and sales within days, but it's also easy to spend thousands on likes and clicks that never turn into customers. Here's a practical starting approach for small businesses.

Boosting vs Ads Manager

The "Boost post" button is quick, but offers limited control. Meta Ads Manager lets you choose the right objective, audience, placements and tracking. For anything beyond a one-off promotion, use Ads Manager.

Choose the right objective

  • Leads: for service businesses wanting enquiries (instant forms or your website).
  • Sales: for online stores, optimised for purchases tracked on your site.
  • Engagement → messages: ads that open a WhatsApp, Messenger or Instagram chat. Very effective in India.
  • Traffic: sends clicks, but not necessarily buyers. Use it carefully.
  • Awareness: rarely the right first choice for a small budget.

Pick the objective that matches the result you want; Meta optimises for exactly what you ask for.

Audiences

  • Location: target only the areas you serve, such as a radius around your shop or specific cities.
  • Broad targeting often works well now, with the ad creative doing the targeting.
  • Custom audiences: website visitors, Instagram engagers, past customers (uploaded with consent).
  • Lookalikes of your best customers, once you have enough data.

Creatives that work

  • Short vertical videos (Reels-style) showing the product, the result or the person behind the business.
  • Real photos over polished stock images.
  • A clear offer and next step: "Free trial class this week. Message us to book."
  • Several versions, so Meta can find the winner. Refresh creatives when results drop.

Where to send people

Don't send ad clicks to your home page. Use a focused landing page or product page that matches the ad exactly, loads fast on mobile and has one clear action. See landing page vs website for ads. For service businesses, click-to-WhatsApp ads are often the simplest high-converting option.

Set up tracking first

Install the Meta Pixel (and ideally the Conversions API) on your website, and track the actions that matter: leads, purchases, WhatsApp clicks. Without tracking, you can't tell which ads make money. Respect privacy rules and mention tracking in your privacy policy; see cookies, consent and the DPDP Act.

Budget

Start with a modest daily budget you're comfortable losing while you learn, for example a few hundred rupees a day per campaign, and run it for at least a week before judging. Measure cost per lead or cost per purchase, not likes or reach. Increase budget gradually on what works.

Common mistakes

  • Changing settings every day before the ad has time to learn.
  • Targeting too many narrow interests on a tiny budget.
  • Slow landing pages that lose people after they click.
  • Not replying quickly to leads and messages.

Meta ads or Google ads?

Meta ads create demand: people see your product while scrolling. Google ads capture demand from people already searching. Many businesses use both; see Google Ads vs SEO and turning Instagram followers into sales.
