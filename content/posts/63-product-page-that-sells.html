The product page is where the buying decision happens. Shoppers arrive from Instagram, Google or an ad, and in a few seconds decide whether to add to cart or go back. Here's what a product page needs to answer, roughly in the order shoppers look.

1. A clear title

Say what it is in plain words: "Handloom Cotton Saree – Indigo with Silver Zari Border". Include the key details people search for (material, colour, size or quantity). Skip vague names like "Midnight Dream" unless the description makes the product obvious.

2. Photos that answer questions

  • A clean main photo, then back, side and detail shots.
  • The product in use or on a person, so shoppers can judge size and fit.
  • A short video where it helps: fabric drape, how a gadget works.

Good photos reduce returns. See taking product photos with your phone.

3. Price, offers and savings

Show the price clearly with taxes included, any discount, and offers like "Free shipping above ₹999" or "10% off on prepaid orders". Don't make shoppers hunt for the real price.

4. Delivery, COD and returns, near the button

Indian shoppers want to know before they commit:

  • Delivery time, ideally with a PIN code checker.
  • COD available? And any COD charge.
  • Returns or exchange: a one-line summary with a link to the full policy.

Putting this right under the Add to Cart button removes the most common hesitation.

5. A clear Add to Cart (and Buy Now)

Make it big, high-contrast and visible without scrolling on mobile. A sticky button on mobile that stays at the bottom of the screen works well for long pages.

6. A useful description

Start with the benefit, then the details:

  • Who it's for and why it's good.
  • Key features as bullet points.
  • Specifications: material, dimensions, weight, ingredients, care instructions, country of origin.
  • What's in the box.

Write your own descriptions. Copying the supplier's text means your page looks like everyone else's to shoppers and to Google.

7. Size and fit help

For clothing and footwear, a size chart in centimetres and inches, with how to measure, plus the model's height and size worn. It's one of the biggest ways to cut returns; see selling clothes online.

8. Reviews and photos from buyers

Star ratings and reviews, ideally with customer photos, build trust fast. Ask buyers for a review a few days after delivery.

9. Trust signals

  • Secure payment icons (UPI, cards).
  • A WhatsApp button for questions; see adding WhatsApp.
  • Your contact details and return policy one tap away.

10. Related products

"You may also like" and "Frequently bought together" help shoppers find alternatives and raise order value.

Speed matters

All of this must load fast on a mid-range phone. Heavy apps and huge images slow product pages down; see speeding up a Shopify store. Then make checkout smooth too, with fewer abandoned carts.
