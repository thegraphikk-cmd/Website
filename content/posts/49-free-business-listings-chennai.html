When Google decides which local businesses to show, it looks beyond your website. It checks whether your business appears consistently across trusted directories and platforms. These mentions, often called citations, help Google trust that you're real and where you say you are. Many of the best ones are free.

Why listings matter

  • Trust signals: consistent details across sites help Google confirm your business.
  • Extra visibility: some customers search directly on Justdial, IndiaMART or Practo.
  • Links back to your website, which help new sites get discovered.

The golden rule: consistent NAP

NAP means Name, Address, Phone. Use exactly the same details everywhere:

  • The same business name, without extra keywords.
  • The same address format, including door number, street, area and PIN code.
  • The same main phone number.
  • The same website address (with https).

Write these down once and copy-paste them into every listing. Mismatched details, such as an old address on one site, confuse Google and customers.

Start with these

Essential for everyone

  • Google Business Profile: the most important by far. See how to add your business to Google Maps.
  • Bing Places: quick to set up, and it can import from Google.
  • Apple Business Connect: puts you on Apple Maps for iPhone users.
  • Facebook Page and Instagram with your full contact details.
  • LinkedIn company page, especially for B2B businesses.

Popular Indian directories

  • Justdial: widely used for local services.
  • Sulekha: strong for home services, education and events.
  • IndiaMART and TradeIndia: for manufacturers, wholesalers and B2B suppliers.

Industry-specific

  • Practo or similar for doctors and clinics.
  • Zomato and Swiggy for restaurants; see restaurant websites.
  • Clutch and GoodFirms for agencies and software firms.
  • Wedding platforms for photographers, caterers and decorators.
  • Your industry association or chamber of commerce member directory.

Filling in a listing properly

  • Choose the most accurate category.
  • Write a short, factual description of your services and areas served.
  • Add real photos and your logo.
  • Add opening hours, and keep them updated for holidays.
  • Link to your website and the most relevant page.

Expect sales calls

Several directories offer free listings and then call regularly to sell paid packages. Paid plans can help in some industries, but you don't need them for the SEO benefit. Decide based on whether that platform actually sends you customers.

What to avoid

  • Mass submission services promising "500 listings for ₹999". Most of those sites are spam and add nothing.
  • Duplicate listings on the same platform. Claim and merge them instead.
  • Fake addresses in areas where you don't operate.

Keep them current

If you move, change your number or rebrand, update every listing, starting with Google. Keep a simple spreadsheet of where you're listed, with the login for each. Listings are one piece of local SEO; our local SEO checklist covers the rest.
