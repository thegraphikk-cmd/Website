Many Indian manufacturers rely on IndiaMART, TradeIndia and personal networks for enquiries. Those platforms work, but buyers comparing suppliers, especially larger companies and overseas buyers, will look for your own website before placing serious orders. A professional B2B site builds credibility and brings enquiries you don't share with fifty competitors.

What B2B buyers look for

  • Products and specifications, in detail.
  • Capabilities: capacity, machinery, materials, tolerances, customisation.
  • Quality and certifications: ISO, BIS, CE or industry-specific approvals.
  • Experience: years in business and industries served.
  • Proof: clients (with permission), case studies and facility photos.
  • How to request a quote, quickly.

Build a proper product catalogue

Instead of one page listing everything, create a page per product or product family with:

  • Clear photos and technical drawings.
  • Specifications in a table: dimensions, materials, grades, finishes, standards.
  • Applications and industries.
  • Minimum order quantity and lead times, if you can share them.
  • Downloadable spec sheets or brochures (PDF), with the key details also on the page.

Product pages with real specifications rank for the exact searches procurement teams make.

Show your capabilities

A capabilities or infrastructure page with your machinery list, production capacity, in-house processes, testing facilities and photos of the factory floor often matters more to B2B buyers than the home page.

Make RFQs easy

  • A Request a Quote form on every product page: company, product, quantity, specifications, delivery location, file upload for drawings.
  • A WhatsApp button for quick questions; see adding WhatsApp.
  • A named contact or sales team with direct phone numbers.
  • Fast responses. B2B enquiries often go to several suppliers at once.

For exporters

  • An English site, plus key languages for your target markets if relevant.
  • Export experience, countries served, Incoterms you work with, and certifications buyers expect.
  • A .com domain often works better for international credibility; see .in vs .com.
  • A professional email on your domain; see business email.

Be found

  • Product and industry pages targeting specific searches ("SS304 pipe fittings manufacturer in Chennai").
  • A Google Business Profile for your factory or office.
  • Keep IndiaMART and directory listings, linking back to your website.
  • Use keyword research to find the terms buyers use.

Keep it professional and fast

Clean design, real photos, accurate specs and a fast site signal a reliable supplier. Outdated designs and broken links signal the opposite. For costs, see how much a website costs in India.
