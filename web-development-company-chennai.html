What we build for Chennai businesses

Business websites

Sites for local businesses that need calls, bookings and enquiries. Up to 50 pages, mobile-first, with WhatsApp chat, click-to-call, an enquiry or appointment form and your Google reviews on the page.

Online stores

Shopify or WooCommerce stores set up for Indian customers: UPI, cards and cash on delivery, shipping rates, GST invoices and product schema so your products can show up in Google search.

Landing pages

Single long-form pages for Google Ads, Meta ads and launches, with a form connected to your CRM and conversion tracking installed.

Custom web development

Sites and features that don't fit a template, such as booking flows and third-party integrations, built in custom code.

How a project runs

  1. Call and proposal (week 1). A 20-minute call in person, on Google Meet or by phone, then a written scope and fixed price.
  2. Design (weeks 1–2). Wireframes and a clickable prototype for desktop and mobile that you review and approve.
  3. Build on staging. A private link that updates as we build, so you can see progress any time.
  4. Testing and launch. We test on real phones and browsers, go live with redirects, analytics and Search Console, and hand over every login.
  5. Support. An included support period after launch, then an optional care plan from ₹4,000 a month.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a website cost in Chennai?

With The Graphikk, a business website starts at ₹40,000 for up to 50 pages with basic SEO and Google Business Profile setup included. Online stores start at ₹50,000 and landing pages at ₹20,000. All prices are plus 18% GST, and you get a fixed written quote after a 20-minute call.

Can we meet in person in Chennai?

Yes. We're based in Chennai and hold kickoff and design reviews at your office or ours. You can also work with us over Google Meet, phone and WhatsApp.

How long does it take to build a website?

A standard business website takes 2–3 weeks. An online store takes 4–5 weeks, and a landing page 1–2 weeks. Larger custom builds take up to eight weeks.

Do you build websites in Tamil?

Yes. We build bilingual Tamil and English websites so every customer can read your site clearly. A Tamil or Hindi version of an existing site is a ₹15,000 add-on.

Which platforms do you use?

Custom code, WordPress, Shopify or WooCommerce, depending on what your business needs. Online stores come with UPI, cards and cash on delivery through Razorpay or your store's payment gateway.

Will my website rank on Google?

Every site is built SEO-ready: clean headings, schema markup, a sitemap, fast load times and Google Search Console set up at launch. For ongoing ranking work, our monthly SEO plan starts at ₹35,000 a month.

Who owns the website after it's built?

You do. Once the project is paid in full, the code, design files, content, domain and hosting accounts are all in your name.
