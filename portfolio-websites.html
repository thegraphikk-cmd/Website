FAQ

Portfolio questions

Do I need a portfolio website if I have LinkedIn or Instagram?+

Social profiles are rented space. A portfolio site on your own domain shows up when people search your name, presents your work the way you choose, and stays yours if a platform changes.

Can I update the portfolio myself?+

Yes. You get an easy editor for adding projects, posts and photos, plus a short video walkthrough.

How long does a portfolio website take?+

One-page portfolios take about a week. Multi-page portfolios with case studies take two to four weeks.

I don't have much work yet. Can I still have one?+

Yes. Students and career changers often start with two or three projects, a strong About section and a downloadable CV. You can add more later.

Can you write my bio and project descriptions?+

Yes. Copywriting is included in Signature and available as an add-on for the other packages.
